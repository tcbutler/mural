Setup

Retract belts

1 Place the belt loops

Hook both belt loops onto the homing screws.

Motors are released — the belts will run out freely and Mural will not hold itself up. Only do this with the machine resting or supported. Press Hold motors before jogging or continuing.

If the belts are fully out, the motors hold too firmly to pull them through by hand — release them, pull the slack in, then hold again.

2 Retract the belts

Run each motor until its belt is retracted and the motor stalls. Don't let a motor stall for long.

If auto-detection fails, use the manual controls below.

Left belt: idle
Right belt: idle

The drawable area is the rectangle Mural can physically reach between its two hanging points. It's 60% of the measured pin distance — the machine reserves a 20% margin on the left, right, and top edges so the pen can safely swing through hard turns. Pen holder calibration
Jog the servo and record three positions. Higher angles raise the pen. Record the lowest and highest angles at which the pen is still held, then the unlocked angle where it can be lifted out — usually only a few degrees above the highest. Going much further risks the mechanism not re-engaging the holder when a pen is put back.

Physics constants
Belt length the firmware assumes at the fully-retracted home position. Anything between the anchor point and the belt end counts here — if the belts are hooked to carabiners rather than straight onto the nails, add their length. Getting this wrong skews the whole coordinate system, worst towards the edges of the drawable area. Note it is a single value applied to both motors, so unequal hardware on the two sides cannot be expressed and will leave a skew no calibration can remove.
